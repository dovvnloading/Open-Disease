# Ethics, safety and scientific limitations

Open-Disease is **not** a healthcare provider, a regulated device, a clinical trial sponsor, an IRB, or a drug discovery laboratory. A dataset, machine learning model, replicated differential-expression signature, gene mapping or cell-model finding is **not** a cure.

## Hard boundaries

- No personal medical records, identifiable participant information, raw clinical sequencing or genotypes in GitHub issues, pull requests, commits, CI logs or artifacts. Do not recruit participants or collect new biospecimens without qualified institutional and ethical oversight.
- Never distribute restricted files, bypass Data Access Committees, or assume open website visibility is an open license. Respect copyright, data-use agreements and patient consent.
- Outputs are not medical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, or evidence supporting human dosing, intervention or trial participation.
- No claims of clinical sensitivity/specificity, safety, efficacy, fertility preservation, disease modification or cure based on simulated results, small studies, observational correlation or a single independent experiment.
- No automated web downloading, patient matching, re-identification, or deployment of diagnostic tools.

## Scientific guardrails

- Genuine independence is a participant/study property, not a distinct filename, GEO ID, library or cell.
- Target discovery must separate genetics-to-gene mapping from expression association and from causal perturbation.
- Report donor counts and attrition by study/phase/cell type, covariate balance, uncertainty and nonreplications. Do not hide negative controls.
- Evaluate potential harms to reproductive biology, immune response, fertility, pregnancy, non-affected tissues and accessibility before selecting a lab hypothesis.
- Recognize severe ascertainment bias in clinical/surgical cohorts, historically excluded demographics, and potential gaps in ancestry, gender identities, age and phenotypes.
- Independent biostatistical and domain reviews are required before describing any molecular target as validated.

## Community

Patient-perspective input can improve future priority-setting, but no recruitment or outreach is required to undertake independent computational work. Do not request anyone's diagnosis, personal contact details or sensitive health history on public GitHub. Share project feedback without personal health information.

## IP and transparency

Our MIT license covers only **original code**. Third-party publications, datasets, patents and analysis code remain subject to their own rights and conditions. The 2026 endometrial atlas article includes disclosure of patent applications relating to some gene signatures. An open-source repository does not grant freedom to operate for a commercial assay or therapy.

## Claims ladder

1. **Software validated on synthetic data** — current milestone.
2. **Published result independently reproduced** — not yet achieved.
3. **Gene hypothesis externally replicated, covariates reviewed** — not yet achieved.
4. **Biological function altered in an appropriate experimental model** — requires partner.
5. **Human therapeutic benefit shown in ethically approved trials** — far beyond this repository.

Every release, manuscript, press statement and grant application should specify which rung was actually demonstrated.
