# 3p21.31 decision rules, 2026-11-16

Issue [#50](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Disease/issues/50). The reported HYAL3 posterior, TWAS directions and failed biopsy score were known before this audit. This is a retrospective audit of a selected locus. No new disease–QTL posterior has been calculated at design time.

1. Reconcile the two published GWAS and identify regional leads, secondary associations, credible sets, eQTL test denominator, tissues and original priors. Reuse prior repository extractions of Koller S8/S13. Inspect disease and fine-mapping sheets and the UK endometrium QTL sheets for the new questions. Characterize coverage, including non-hits. Keep checksums for acquired files.
2. Use chr3:48,652,148–50,652,148 inclusive on GRCh37 (±1 Mb around the reported MTAG marker rs1352889). This physical anchor does not assign it univariate lead status. Use the orthologous reference-aligned interval for GRCh38. Exact reproduction requires the authors' actual variant set, EUR clinical GWAS, GTEx v8 cis-eQTL release and HyPrColoc options. Label changed data, phenotype, region or model as sensitivity analyses.
3. Compare disease with each of HYAL3, RBM6, TMEM115 and the previously reported neighbor GMPPB. Enumerate every additional assayed cis gene in the interval under the same quality filters. Preselected comparator: sigmoid colon HYAL3 for cross-tissue sharing. Where a full endometrial resource is available, use VEZT at its published locus as a pipeline positive control. Report untested comparators.
4. Require public, unthresholded regional GWAS and QTL statistics with documented ancestry, phenotype, build, unique normalized chromosome-position-REF-ALT, effect orientation, beta/SE or compatible signed Z and variance, sample sizes and frequency information sufficient for QC. Use one-to-one build mapping and check reference bases. Exclude unmapped, conflicting or unresolved duplicate/multiallelic records and ambiguous A/T or C/G variants without decisive frequency orientation. Report each attrition reason, overlap by gene and coverage of credible-set variants. Stop inference if disease peaks or high-posterior candidates are lost, or tables are filtered on significance.
5. On eligible data, compare coloc.abf H0–H4 (including distinct signals H3) at p1=p2=1e-4 and p12=1e-6, 1e-5, 5e-5. This is a single-signal challenge separate from exact HyPrColoc reproduction. For multiple signals, require separate study-matched signed LD in identical variant order and orientation, check PSD, diagonals, symmetry and Z–LD consistency, then run SuSiE-RSS/coloc.susie per signal with maximum effects 5 and 10. Compare ancestry-matched public aggregate LD as a sensitivity where permitted. Missing LD blocks only the multi-signal arm.
6. A gene-specific lead requires distinguishable evidence for gene and compartment in independent endometrium, stability across prior and multi-signal checks, and adequately measured regional competitors. Several credible genes, inadequate coverage or unresolved tissue preserve uncertainty. Record the scientific decision separately from which computations ran.

Limit to the two publisher workbooks, source metadata and specifically identified, rights-compatible public regional summary/LD slices within budget. Keep workbooks local. Exclude participant-level data, external contact, paid compute and genome-wide downloads.
