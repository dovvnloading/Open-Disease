## Linkage disequilibrium and gates for inference

Pan-UK Biobank supplies ancestry-specific covariate-adjusted LD matrices. Weissbrod et al. also release British-ancestry UK Biobank LD in overlapping 3-Mb blocks from approximately 337,000 people. Their aggregate matrices are plausible EUR reference sensitivities after verification of lawful bounded access, variant coverage, effect-allele orientation, sample ancestry, variant order and correlation signs. Marginal allele frequencies, LD scores and pairwise squared correlations cannot replace a signed matrix. No public in-sample signed LD for the exact EUR clinical meta-analysis and each QTL cohort was verified here. UK Biobank contributes to the GWAS, so such a reference would not be independent evidence of disease association. [Pan-UKB definitions](https://pan.ukbb.broadinstitute.org/docs/ld/index.html) · [Weissbrod release](https://registry.opendata.aws/ukbb-ld/)
