# Portability of the Indian endometriosis discovery loci
Drafted 2026-11-15 before examining external variant effects. Exploratory, retrospective source selection. We saw the 21 Indian discovery results including the IL37 annotation before choosing the question. Compare the full fixed 21 suggestive risk variants in Table 2 of Anand et al., DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-69642-9, with GCST90205183, the 2023 endometriosis GWAS (non-23andMe public analysis). Verify sources and composition before interpreting non-overlap. This evaluates portability of the exact tag, not causality of the closest gene or transportability of all disease risk. Use the original 21-variant denominator with Bonferroni two-sided 0.05/21 and matching discovery risk direction. Report all rows, exact sign-concordance test where harmonizable, and directional binomial P, labelled exploratory. If alleles do not agree or an A/T or C/G variant is strand-ambiguous with incomplete allele-frequency evidence, exclude it in the primary analysis and retain its slot in correction. Prefer effect estimates and 95% confidence intervals when supported by source. Report missingness, positive controls from known association loci (WNT4 and CDKN2B-AS1) identified by Anand et al., outside the family. Bounded public academic aggregate-only download, 200 MB and 30 minutes. No participant-level records, no restricted or paid resources.

Implementation notes before inspecting external effects: the publisher's supplement provides both alleles and GRCh38 positions in S2 and separate reference-population risk allele frequencies in S4. Verify rsID, chromosome, GRCh38 position and allele pair for all exact matches, including rs56991723 = chr7:74321005:G:A from Table 2/S2. Keep external P for an unresolved orientation as unsigned only. Orient A/T or C/G only when the risk frequency in both European and Indian references lies on the same side of 0.5, both at least 0.10 away from 0.5, and validation risk frequency under the proposed alignment is within 0.10 of S4 European frequency; do not impute an indel or an LD proxy. Use rs10917151 (A, WNT4) and rs10122243 (T, CDKN2B-AS1), the previously established tags in S5, to verify signs/performance outside the tested family. The validation dataset helped nominate these controls; use them as pipeline checks only.

Before completing the validation file download, specify one interpretation aid: give simultaneous two-sided Wald confidence intervals on external risk-allele OR using z = Φ⁻¹(1 − 0.05/(2×21)). Count how many intervals fall entirely inside (1/1.10,1.10), and how many upper bounds fall below the discovery point OR. The first is a sensitivity threshold, not a biological or clinical cutoff; the second is descriptive given discovery selection. The ordinary 95% intervals remain marginal.

A search for a closer population comparison found a second, secondary sensitivity source, Pan-UK Biobank female phecode 615 (94 Central/South Asian cases, 3,892 controls, heritability QC flag h2_z_insignificant). Before opening its variant results: query all exact 21 leads, including absent and strand-ambiguous, using the same 21-slot correction. Exclude low-confidence variants and results with an uncertain allele mapping. This cohort uses a broader phenotype than the Indian surgical cohort. It will be interpreted only as a low-powered sensitivity analysis. The UKB may overlap GCST90205183 at study level, so do not pool these results or call the two external analyses independent replications.
