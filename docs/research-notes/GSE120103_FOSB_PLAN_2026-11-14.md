# Prospective FOSB comparison in secretory endometrium

November 14, 2026 UTC. This plan freezes the cohort, feature, normalization, contrast and decision before inspecting its expression values. The earlier Rome study's positive but inconclusive result is known. This cohort was selected on recruitment, tissue, hormone-cycle phase, fertility groups, public availability and target annotation. The UCSF endpoint supplied the expected positive direction. [Issue #28](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Disease/issues/28) · [Original endpoint](GSE51981_FOSB_ENDPOINT_2026-11-13.md) · [Rome result](GSE25628_FOSB_RESULTS_2026-11-13.md)

## Participants and groups

Use all 36 single-channel GPL6480 endometrial arrays from AIIMS New Delhi. Bhat et al.'s Table 1 reports nine patients contributing to the microarray experiment in each of four cells, fertile or infertile women with stage IV ovarian endometriosis and the corresponding study controls. Each array represents a patient by this published design. GEO titles call the numbered arrays replicates. No individual donor crosswalk is available. Enrollment is distinct from Rome, UCSF and Turku by institution. Fertile controls attended for sterilization or hysterectomy for nonuterine indications. Infertile controls attended the infertility clinic, with surgical exclusion of occult disease unresolved. Individual hormone history and secretory substage remain unresolved. The study was designed around a different question. [Primary paper, Methods and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6375207/) · [GEO study](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE120103)

Require concordant clinical group in title and sample-group characteristic, tissue endometrium, secretory source record and group codes 1A, 2A, 1B and 2B in their expected cells. GEO source-name prose incorrectly repeats “Fertile” in group 2B. Accept this exact documented discrepancy only for that code because the title, sample-group characteristic and publication agree on the infertile affected arm. Require nine distinct arrays per cell, unique within-cell replicate numbers 1 through 9 and agreement between sample metadata and matrix columns. Do not infer additional donor links.

## Measurement

Use only Agilent GPL6480 feature A_23_P429998, uniquely annotated as FOSB and Entrez 2354 in the pinned GEO platform annotation (gzip SHA-256 aacec81b2d95f183f0827b43e769388a92917811b81920c13ecc8fe148c5a3ab). GEO labels the submitted VALUE as normalized signal intensity. The authors describe log2 transformation and 75th-percentile normalization for their published Y-chromosome analyses. The archived matrix's units and normalization stage are ambiguous. Use a within-array percentile, invariant to increasing transformations of that array, to measure FOSB's relative position among assayed features. A percentile difference is a change in relative position and cannot be interpreted as a fold change.

Use the intersection of unique matrix features with finite submitted intensities on all 36 arrays, including the target. Treat blank, NA, NaN, null and nonfinite numeric values as missing. Reject malformed tokens or duplicated or empty feature keys. Require at least 20,000 complete features, the same common reference universe for all arrays, exactly one target row, and a complete gzip and matrix terminator. For each array compute 100 × (number of common features strictly below FOSB plus half the number equal to FOSB) / number of common features. Include FOSB in the tie count. Report the overall number of complete and excluded features. This relative measurement can move when other features change.

## Contrast and decision

The primary contrast weights fertility strata equally. Compute the case minus control mean percentile in each stratum and average the two differences. Report both stratum differences descriptively. For each of the four cells let v equal its sample variance divided by four times its sample size. The contrast standard error is the square root of the sum of v. Use Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, (sum v) squared divided by the sum of v squared / (cell size minus one). Report the two-sided 95% t interval and p-value. The independent units are women under the source design. The interval is approximate with nine women per cell. Stop if the variance of the contrast cannot be estimated.

An interval above zero supports the expected direction **for relative FOSB position in this cohort**. An interval below zero supports the opposite direction. An interval containing zero is inconclusive. Report the range of the primary contrast after omitting each array while retaining equal stratum weights. No alternate probe, chosen subgroup, pooled cross-cohort effect or disease-wide significance claim. Source, measurement or design failures produce a documented blocker. No seed is needed. Verify the aggregate arithmetic independently, keep person-level materials local and record exact source digests and run versions.
