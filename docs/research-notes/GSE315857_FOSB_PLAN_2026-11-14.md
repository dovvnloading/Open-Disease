## Frozen endpoint and interpretation

Within each sample use all 22,961 named transcript rows, including FOSB, with no filtering on expression. Exclude the four unnamed rows from this shared reference. Rank in ascending order, using the mean of one-based ranks for tied values. Calculate `100 × (midrank − 1) / (22,961 − 1)`. The score can move through changes in FOSB or in the background transcripts and is invariant to a strictly increasing within-sample transformation.

Report the case median minus the control median in percentile points, and the two group medians. For even group sizes use the mean of the middle pair. Also report the minimum and maximum of the eight case-minus-control median differences after deleting each participant once. Use the exact unrounded primary contrast for direction: positive agrees descriptively with the prespecified direction, zero is no observed difference, and negative is descriptively opposite. Give no p-value, confidence interval or population-level directional claim. The leave-one-out range measures sensitivity to a single participant and supplies no sampling interval. Keep all eight included in the primary result irrespective of the sensitivity range.
