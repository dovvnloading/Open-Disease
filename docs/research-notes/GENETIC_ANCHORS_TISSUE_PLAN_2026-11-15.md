Exploratory secondary analysis, November 15, 2026. The previous ten expression nominations and their unsuccessful validation are public. The gene family comes exclusively from the endometrium eQTL rows of Rahmioglu et al. Table 1. The five are LNC-LBCS (100506885), TRA2A (29896), VEZT (55591), SRP14 (6727) and HOXB9 (3219). ## Paired tissue comparison

Use GSE7305 and GSE11691, two human paired lesion and uterine-biopsy series with separate recruitment and different platforms. Verify source-declared within-person pairing from series or curated GEO DataSet fields. Never infer pairs from accession or column order. Map features with a unique symbol and numeric Entrez assignment using GEO's corresponding platform annotation. Rank finite features together within each specimen, using 100 × (midrank − 0.5) / number of features. Keep the common finite feature reference inside a study and average all eligible probes per gene. Check complete platforms, source checksums, unique donor tissues and one contrast per patient. Record missing or ambiguous targets. Calculate lesion minus the same woman's uterine expression percentile. This comparison includes normal differences between anatomical sites and between their cell populations.

Test both directions using exact flips of the sign of each paired difference, keeping ties and observed assignments. For each gene and direction take the larger of its two study one-sided p-values. Double the smaller of the two directional maxima, capped at one. This asks whether a common direction is supported in both cohorts. Correct across the original five genes with Holm at 0.05, assigning an unmeasured gene p=1 for bookkeeping. Report every estimate, one-sided and two-sided counts, number of pairs positive/negative, and omit-one-pair range. As a robustness check calculate exact sign tests that ignore magnitudes, and report probe-level direction consistency. Report platform-specific unranked log2 differences only where the processed scale is documented.

## Uterine disease-state comparison

Extract the same five genes from the Finnish seven-stratum aggregate screen. Reuse the audited donor-level Rome and Mumbai scorers and the published Melbourne high-detection table. Test common direction between Finland and Rome only as a separate secondary family. Because the Finnish public summary is two-sided, use the maximum of both studies' two-sided p-values when signs agree, otherwise one, then five-gene Holm. Interpret this as a two-study nonzero-effect screen with observed sign agreement. Report Mumbai effect and exact two-sided p, and all Melbourne mapped probe p-values with within-gene Bonferroni. Melbourne cannot supply independently verified direction. Never pool rank differences across platforms or infer a tissue interaction from separate p-values. Only safe aggregates and offline code enter Git.
