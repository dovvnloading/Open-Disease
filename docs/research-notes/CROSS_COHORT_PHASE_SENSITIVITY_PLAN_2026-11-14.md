# Secondary diagnostic for the five pathway direction reversals

This diagnostic was specified after observing the complete locked Rome test. All five nominated pathways rose in Finland and fell in Rome. Rome contains only proliferative biopsies, whereas the Finnish screen weighted four proliferative and three secretory run-by-phase strata together. The diagnostic asks a narrower question: does the sign in Finnish proliferative tissue alone agree with the sign in Rome? The primary validation decision and its ten tests are complete.

Retain every locked candidate, the original score definitions, eligible Finnish donors, exact within-run groups and the original Rome contrast. Within each Finnish phase take the unweighted mean of its within-run case-minus-control contrasts. Report both phase-specific contrasts, their stratum counts, and simple concordance with Rome. As an integrity check require the pooled Finnish effect to equal (4 × proliferative + 3 × secretory) / 7 for every candidate and reproduce the original Finnish and Rome directions. Report the number of reversed pathways whose Finnish proliferative sign is still positive. Do not replace features, fit interactions, retest the opposite direction, or give new p-values. This is a descriptive comparison prompted by the outcomes; it cannot assign discrepancies to phase, clinical mix, cell proportions or measurement differences.
