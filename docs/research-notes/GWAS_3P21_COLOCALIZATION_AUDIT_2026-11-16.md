# What endometriosis genetics establishes at chromosome 3p21.31

**Decision — INCONCLUSIVE for a specific disease gene or regulatory mechanism.** The region carries inherited susceptibility, and rs13100173-A is a strong, directly verified HYAL3 expression allele in bulk uterus and sigmoid colon. The 2023 disease lead closely tags the 2026 cross-ancestry fine-mapping candidate in European reference haplotypes. Both tag the clinical HYAL3 candidate poorly. Existing results leave open whether separate disease effects, one imperfectly tagged effect, or differences between studies explain the pattern. No independent colocalization posterior met the frozen eligibility rules. [Rahmioglu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-023-01323-z) · [Koller et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-026-02582-2) · [Six pairwise LD results](../../evidence/gwas-3p21-colocalization-audit/four_marker_ld.json)

rs13100173 had the lowest HYAL3 eQTL P in the frozen interval in **both** tissues. This verifies the direction and strength of the molecular association in the original GTEx resource. Koller's HyPrColoc model assumes at most one causal variant per trait per region. The publication selected PPFC >0.7 and SNP contribution >0.5. Neither number is the probability of disease mediation. The same clinical GWAS returned HYAL3 in six tissues, including uterus, ovary and sigmoid colon. GTEx shares donors across tissues. Only RBM6 and GMPPB meet the authors' threshold of 3.72×10⁻⁶. Correlated predictors can yield several associations from the same regional variants. ## What did the independent test establish?

1. **Exact clinical reproduction.** The Catalog entry for GCST90841386 records 8,334,944 variants and flags full statistics as unavailable there. None of the nine files in the verified author deposit is the exact European clinical GWAS. The README explicitly adds IEGC to the public European no-23andMe product. The stored S8 table is a selected set of 29 rows and supplies insufficient data to refit its model. [Release, readme and Catalog receipts](../../evidence/gwas-3p21-colocalization-audit/disease_release_evidence.json)
2. **Changed-input single-signal sensitivity.** We read 2,857 unthresholded regional rows from Rahmioglu's public GCST90205183, a 23andMe-excluded, mixed-ascertainment sample of 470,866 European and 3,294 Japanese participants. rs1352889-T had β +0.0404 (SE 0.0151, P 0.00746). rs13100173-A had β +0.0080 (SE 0.0113, P 0.479). No regional P crossed 5×10⁻⁸. The smallest P was 1.081×10⁻⁴ at rs113660021. This verifies what the changed public release contains. It cannot refute the distinct clinical result. [Disease slice and provenance](../../evidence/gwas-3p21-colocalization-audit/disease_release_evidence.json)
3. **Coverage gate.** We aligned both traits to GRCh38 and retained 2,469 disease variants after excluding 388 unresolved palindromes. All 341 disease credible-set IDs were present before disease QC and 300 after it. We examined 222,233 nominal uterine rows (108 genes) and 225,236 sigmoid rows (109 genes). Every gene had at least one aligned variant. The public GWAS peak at chr3:48,677,812 had **zero molecular rows in either tissue**. HYAL3 retained 1,525 and 1,528 variants respectively, including 232/341 credible-set IDs in each tissue. Under the original rule requiring disease peak coverage, zero genes qualified. No H0–H4, prior grid or conditional posterior was calculated. The sigmoid comparator failed the same gate. VEZT required a qualifying independent endometrial source. No matched signed disease/QTL LD matrices were acquired. [Per-gene exclusions and all outcomes](../../evidence/gwas-3p21-colocalization-audit/sensitivity.json) · [Frozen design](../../evidence/gwas-3p21-colocalization-audit/design.md)
4. **Independent tissue.** GTEx uterus includes myometrium. Evaluable lining was uncommon and viable lining generally represented inactive basalis. In the independent UK endometrium release (163 donors), we found 61,086 rows for 35 genes in the interval. All 35 genes have a row at rs1352889, including the four focal genes. Neither coordinate field identifies rs13100173. The sheets omit allele orientation and SE. At the lead, squared slope divided by the column labeled “nominal P-value” was almost constant across all 35 genes. For all four focal genes the source flags the largest value of that column as the best hit. We did not interpret it as a P-value or reconstruct an SE. This is missing information in a real tissue comparator. The Australian and other published resources, their cohort reuse and their coverage are documented in the inventory. [UK S34–35 audit](../../evidence/gwas-3p21-colocalization-audit/rahmioglu_workbook.json) · [GTEx histopathology](https://doi.org/10.5858/arpa.2023-0468-OA) · [Data inventory](../../evidence/gwas-3p21-colocalization-audit/inventory.md)

Earlier Open-Disease work tested anchor transfer, a fixed three-gene score, tissue contrasts and portability. The score failed its cross-cohort rule. These analyses address a different question and were not rerun. The initial public design preceded new computation, and the later six-pair and S31 checks were specified after reading the workbooks. This audit is exploratory aggregate-data computation at an already selected locus. ## One experiment that would change the decision

**Condition the clinical disease association and compartment-specific expression associations on the regional signals, then test colocalization per signal and gene.** The first required input is the unthresholded European clinical interval from GCST90841386, with effect alleles, effects and uncertainty for **all** variants spanning both marker groups. Pair it with complete independent, cycle-staged endometrial cis-eQTLs and histologically verified myometrial eQTLs, with signed, allele-matched LD for each trait. Verify donor overlap and power. Use the existing frozen priors and multicausal models on every adequately measured regional gene. A clinical component that remains associated after conditioning on the BSN-tagged component and shares only with a well-measured endometrial gene would justify studying that gene further. Sharing across genes or compartments, loss after conditioning, or inadequate coverage leaves the mechanism unresolved.
