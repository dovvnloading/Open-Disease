# Can the Finnish collection qualify the peritoneal biopsies?

**NO-GO for the pathology-controlled comparison in GSE141549.** The public evidence verifies the endometriosis diagnosis in case donors and laparoscopic exclusion in control donors. The Finnish investigators also describe how they examined lesion sections. The missing observations are a microscopic finding for each assayed peritoneal comparison biopsy and comparable sampling sites across the two donor groups. A biopsy can look normal during surgery while its microscopic status remains unknown. This literature and metadata assessment on November 16, 2026 used no five-gene expression outcomes. The public Figshare history lists one version. Its manifest contains the database archive, a qPCR sample-count workbook and three figures. The archived web application includes a lesion field on the histology form. Its code requests a lesion-histopathology vocabulary. In the released database that vocabulary has zero entries, and all 163 specimen-linked pathology rows belong to endometrium. The three arms contain 71 superficial lesion specimens from 50 donors, 34 case peritoneal specimens from 34 donors and 24 control peritoneal specimens from 24 donors. Zero specimens in each arm join to a pathology row. Seventeen case donors supplied both lesion and peritoneum, so the first two donor totals overlap. **What would reopen the Finnish comparison.** An eligible public release would link a study specimen key to its lesion-positive or lesion-negative microscopy finding and pelvic subsite, with the operative sampling rule or distance from visible disease where recorded. The existing specimen, donor and run keys would then allow the eligible people and within-run comparisons to be counted before looking at the score. The surgeon forms and formalin aliquots identify possible origins for that information. Their completeness and their linkage to any individual expression assay remain unknown.
