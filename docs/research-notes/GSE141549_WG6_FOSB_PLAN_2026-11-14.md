## Estimator, uncertainty and decision

Within each shared run-by-phase stratum calculate the mean percentile in cases minus the mean in controls. The primary effect is the equally weighted average of these stratum differences in percentile points. Its target is the average shared study stratum. The design supplies one eligible assay per donor. Record the range of the primary effect after deleting each entire stratum in turn and retaining equal weights for the remainder. Report only aggregate results.

Use a two-sided exact stratified permutation test. Enumerate every assignment of individual donor labels that preserves the original case and control counts separately within each stratum. For each assignment, recompute the same equally weighted statistic. The p-value is the fraction with absolute permuted statistic greater than or equal to the absolute observed statistic, including the observed assignment and every tie. Use integer doubled midranks and integer or exact rational contrasts for comparison. Report total assignments, extreme assignments and exact p-value. No random seed or pseudocount applies. The test supplies no confidence interval for a heterogeneous mean effect.
