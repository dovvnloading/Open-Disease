# Prospective genome-wide and Reactome screen with external validation

November 14, 2026. The project previously tested FOSB in four cohorts. This study asks whether any other human gene or annotated pathway has a reproducible directional difference in uterine endometrium in two separately recruited cohorts. The earlier FOSB results and cohort limitations were known. No new disease-group results from this screen were available when the plan was written. This is a retrospective observational analysis with prospectively fixed computations. [Issue #33](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Disease/issues/33) · [Finnish census](GSE141549_CHIP_CENSUS_2026-11-14.md) · [Rome cohort](GSE25628_FOSB_RESULTS_2026-11-13.md)

## Cohorts and held-out analysis

Discover in the exact 25 Finnish WG-6 assays, 12 cases and 13 controls, in the seven run-by-histologic-phase cells of the [verified Finnish design](GSE141549_WG6_FOSB_PLAN_2026-11-14.md). Use the native assay foreign key, one clinically eligible assay per donor, complete 48,701-address manufacturer match and native avg_signal. The database's medication field defaults to false and specifies no washout. Validate in GSE25628's eight proliferative eutopic case biopsies and six proliferative surgical controls in Rome. Exclude all paired lesion arrays. One uterine sample per woman follows the study design. Recent hormone treatment, slide allocation and some other pelvic pathology are unresolved. Recruitment is separate at the study level. The Finnish phases and control indications differ from Rome's. Use only platform annotations from Rome until the discovery nominations, directions and family definitions are committed publicly. The known historical FOSB examination is the only prior project unblinding relevant to this analysis.

## Measurements and candidate universe

For each array rank all platform features in ascending order with ties averaged. Express the score as 100 × (one-based midrank − 0.5) / reference count. Finland requires one finite signal at every physical address per included assay. For Rome use the common finite reference across the 14 included arrays (minimum 20,000). Require complete values on every mapped probe used for the locked gene universe. Fail on disagreement with the locked gene IDs or covered pathway memberships. Average all eligible probe scores for an Entrez gene within each donor. Keep valid human RefSeq probes with a single positive numeric Entrez assignment on WG-6. On GPL571 use non-AFFX probes with a single symbol and single positive numeric Entrez assignment. This cross-platform ID join uses pinned source annotation. Outdated or unmatched identifiers are left unmapped. The analyzed gene family is the intersection of the two platform-annotation universes, excluding Entrez 2354 (previously studied FOSB). Different arrays have different reference features, so effect sizes describe relative ranks within each platform. No mapping is selected using disease labels.

Use Reactome release 98's human NCBI-to-all-pathways annotation, human pathway catalogue and official hierarchy. Remove the Disease chapter R-HSA-1643685 and all descendants, including nodes with multiple parents. Retain both TAS and IEA human memberships with positive numeric gene IDs. Require 15–200 unique shared eligible genes inclusive and at least 50% coverage of all unique numeric source genes for each pathway. Count FOSB in the source denominator when present. Collapse identical covered memberships to the smallest numeric Reactome ID. The pathway score is the within-donor unweighted mean of its covered gene scores. This tests a directional shift in relative transcript abundance and can miss oppositely regulated modules. The scores do not measure enzyme activity or pathway activation. Attribute Reactome as CC BY 4.0. [Reactome downloads](https://reactome.org/download-data) · [Reactome license](https://reactome.org/license)

The annotation-only preflight produced 12,045 shared genes and 12,044 after FOSB exclusion, with 1,049 eligible distinct pathway memberships. Require these exact counts. The SHA-256 of the eligible pathways in ascending numeric ID order, encoded as compact UTF-8 JSON arrays of [stable ID, name, ascending gene IDs] with unescaped Unicode, is `96143a21a5e0b313b74b1a7b37d431c486b0c06d21191fa88e4cf2a477e3e494`.

## Discovery, multiplicity and nomination

Analyze the gene and pathway families separately. For each feature, compute the case-minus-control mean inside each Finnish stratum, then the unweighted mean of those seven differences. Let V be the sum, over strata, of the sample variance of all donors' scores in that stratum times (1/n_case + 1/n_control), divided by the squared number of strata. This is the exact permutation variance for that feature under within-stratum label exchangeability. Use the signed contrast divided by sqrt(V) to rank effects. Zero-variance features have score zero, p=1 and cannot be nominated. No variance shrinkage or gene selection by expression magnitude.

Enumerate all 12,096 assignments preserving the case count in each stratum, synchronized across all features. For each feature report a two-sided raw permutation p-value and a single-step maxT p-value comparing its absolute statistic with the largest absolute statistic in its family at each assignment. Include observed assignments and ties using absolute tolerance 1e-10 on standardized statistics. For the two prespecified families use maxT p ≤ 0.025 per family for a discovery-screen hit. This calibration tests a joint sharp null under exchangeability. Strong family-wise control under partial nulls additionally needs subset pivotality. Shared genes, coexpression and overlapping pathways are preserved in every permutation.

Nominate up to five nonconstant genes and five nonconstant pathways irrespective of whether the discovery threshold is reached. Order by descending absolute standardized statistic rounded to 10 decimal places, then ascending numeric identifier. Skip a pathway if the Jaccard overlap of its covered genes with any already nominated pathway is at least 0.50. Fix each candidate's direction from the unrounded Finnish contrast. Store the ordered nomination, directions, discovery statistics and digests of both complete candidate universes in a standalone public commit before calculating Rome outcomes. No replacements or alternate cutoffs after nomination.

## External decision and reporting

In Rome estimate the unstratified mean case-minus-control difference for each nominated feature using the same shared genes and pathway membership. Test only the locked direction, with all 3,003 assignments preserving eight cases and six controls. Count candidate-direction statistics at least as large as observed, including ties. Use standardized units with the same 1e-10 tie tolerance, p=1 for zero variance. Holm-adjust the one-sided p-values over all nominated genes and pathways together, up to ten tests. Report both effects, discovery raw/maxT values, validation raw/Holm values, direction agreement and the Rome contrast range after omitting each participant. Report all candidates even if every one fails.

Call a candidate cross-cohort supported in this screen only if discovery maxT ≤ 0.025, its Rome effect has the prespecified sign, and Rome Holm p ≤ 0.05. Describe validation-only hits separately and retain both no-hit and discordant results. The two cohorts have different technical and clinical structures. A positive result would be a replicated computational association. A mechanistic or novelty claim needs additional evidence and a candidate-specific prior-literature check. Publish family-wide aggregate discovery results and all nomination outcomes. Individual ranks, scores, intermediate matrices and clinical records stay local. Independently recompute permutation arithmetic; verify the complete null size, candidate order, timing of commits and multiplicity denominators. No pooled effect, expression-driven switches, paid compute or external outreach.

The analysis uses the previously cached Finnish and Rome inputs and at most 130 MB of new public annotation, 4 GB RAM and 120 minutes. No random procedures are required.
