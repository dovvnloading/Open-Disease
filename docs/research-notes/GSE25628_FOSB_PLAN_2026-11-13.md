# Prospective FOSB direction check in the Italian surgical cohort

November 13, 2026 (US Eastern). This plan for [issue #24](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Disease/issues/24) was frozen before inspecting GSE25628 expression values. The question is whether the FOSB difference reported in the UCSF series has the same direction in separately recruited, proliferative-phase surgical samples. The original untreated, pathology-free GSE51981 analysis remains blocked. [Original FOSB endpoint](GSE51981_FOSB_ENDPOINT_2026-11-13.md)

**Study population.** Crispi et al. describe eight affected women contributing one uterine and one lesion biopsy each, and six controls contributing one uterine biopsy each. All microarray biopsies were collected in the proliferative phase. Include the eight eutopic case and six control arrays. Exclude all eight lesion arrays. Each included woman contributes one uterine biopsy according to the study design. The submitter's donor characteristic distinguishes groups and supplies no unique participant keys. Cross-study independence is supported by recruitment in Rome versus the UCSF tissue bank and is assessed at the study level. No participant matching is performed. [Italian study, Methods](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcp.24358) · [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE25628) · [UCSF cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4239429/)

**Applicability.** The control women underwent surgery for benign conditions. These controls cannot establish a contrast against women free of other pelvic pathology. Individual treatment history is unresolved. This question conditions on neither prior hormone use nor absence of other pelvic pathology. The original stricter comparison is a different estimand.

**Endpoint and family.** Use only the prespecified GPL571 probe `202768_at`. The GEO platform annotation dated August 9, 2016 identifies it uniquely as FOSB, Entrez 2354. Its gzip SHA-256 is `9f5a482f9f3d478b47fc80124600319e569f1e9ab447f3f256d61b1fbacce4fa`. Calculate the mean submitted RMA log2 intensity in cases minus the mean in controls and its two-sided, unpaired Welch 95% confidence interval and p-value. Report the ratio of geometric mean intensities as 2 raised to the difference. The family contains one prespecified probe and one contrast. As a sensitivity measure, report the range of the difference after omitting each included array in turn. The sensitivity measure does not change the primary decision.

**Decision.** A positive difference with the interval entirely above zero supports directional agreement in this cohort. A negative difference with the interval entirely below zero supports the opposite direction. Otherwise report an inconclusive result and the observed direction separately. No alternative genes, dose-response models, covariate searches or combined cohorts. The prior paper's larger positive estimate is a reference, not a target magnitude. Small-group Welch intervals rely on independence of women and approximate within-group normality.

**Integrity and stops.** Require the GSE25628/GPL571 header, 22 unique arrays and aligned matrix columns, the audited three tissue/disease groups and sizes, authors' design, documented log2 RMA processing, unique target annotation and exactly one complete numeric target row for the 14 included arrays. Check the complete gzip payload. Fail closed if a required field or target value cannot be verified. Keep individual data under ignored paths. The 24 MB total public-data budget covers the processed matrix, GPL571 annotation and sample metadata. No paid compute.
