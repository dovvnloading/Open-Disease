# A distributed endometriosis expression contrast in independent uterine biopsies

This exploratory analysis was specified after reading the earlier Finnish screen, ten Rome nominations, and five-pathway results in Mumbai. The sorted Finnish weights and published nomination membership already exist. The native Rome CEL headers were inspected for scanner and date balance before computing these new genome-wide score outcomes. The analysis is not a prospectively registered replication.

Use every available gene in the 12,044-gene Finnish screen with its published signed permutation-standardized statistic as a weight. For each external biopsy, multiply that gene’s within-biopsy expression percentile by the Finnish weight, sum, and divide by the sum of the absolute weights over its cohort’s measured overlap. A higher case mean supports transfer in the Finnish direction. Avoid thresholding, tuning and fitting external labels. One score per woman retains covariance between all gene measurements.
