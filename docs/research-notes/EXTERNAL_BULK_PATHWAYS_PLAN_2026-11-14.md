## Decisions and diagnostics

Report the case-minus-control difference in means for each cohort separately in within-sample percentile points. For Mumbai enumerate all 35 assignments preserving the sample counts. The two-sided exact reference includes every absolute difference at least as extreme as observed within 1e-10 points. At p ≤ 0.05 the overall mean passes the joint test under within-cohort label exchangeability. Individual support requires both the global gate and a five-pathway Holm-adjusted two-sided p ≤ 0.05. The first Holm threshold (0.01) is smaller than 1/35. The test addresses a joint pathway direction regardless of which earlier study it matches. Keep assay-specific rank denominators. Do not pool cohorts or treat matching signs as independent tests. For the U.S. study give estimates and the size of the date-conditioned assignment space without a nominal disease p-value.

Seek explicit physical flowcell/run evidence for Mumbai, separately from SRA accession uniqueness. If documented batches fully separate diagnosis, label the 35-assignment reference descriptive and withhold inferential support. If partial overlap is verifiable, also give a descriptive equal-batch contrast and its exact design size within mixed batches. Missing physical batch keys leave exchangeability unverified. Report which limitations apply.

For both cohorts report three prespecified robustness checks for all six endpoints. (1) Omit one donor at a time and show the minimum and maximum primary contrast. (2) Restrict the common members further to the genes measured in the Swiss study, retaining all nonempty sets and giving their denominators. (3) In Mumbai, rank all original transcripts first and average ranks within each mapped gene. In the U.S. use median probe ranks within each gene. These alternate definitions do not replace the primary one. For the Mumbai global score, report a 95% compatibility set by inverting the same unblocked two-sided test under a constant additive score shift. Label its assumption and allow unbounded limits.

Before interpreting an individual subset, show the numerical resolution of the design and the overlap between the nested FGFR3 pathways. Compare these new results with the earlier point estimates without claiming donor-level causation, diagnostic utility, or cell-type specificity. Verify from source with another implementation. Publish every prespecified endpoint and only aggregate study outputs. Input checks and annotations may be shared between implementations if disclosed. All assignments are exhaustive. No random seed.
