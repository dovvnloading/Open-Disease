# Separate Melbourne summary-statistic check of the five locked genes

November 14, 2026. The Finland and Rome results are known. This third study is a new external follow-up specified before reading the Melbourne statistics for the five candidate genes. The original two-cohort decision is final. The known paper-level result was that no disease difference passed the authors’ genome-wide correction. A targeted test could still produce different evidence. ## Population and eligible endpoint

Use Fung et al.'s Table S20 for the combined Melbourne cohorts, the complete 15,262-probe high-detection expression analysis adjusted for menstrual cycle stage by limma. The study covers 229 women at two Melbourne clinics. Of 165 surgical patients, 112 were recorded as cases, 48 as controls and five unknown. Of 64 IVF patients, 21 were recorded as cases, 41 as controls and two unknown. Surgical diagnoses were made at laparoscopy and IVF diagnoses were self-reported. These are enrollment counts. The model sample size and handling of unknown disease status must be described to the extent documented. The public table contains only probe-level coefficients and tests. Neither condition-matched patient data nor cell proportions are available from this table. Do not count a later publication using the same Melbourne clinics as a separate independent validation.

Use only the exact five names and original directions from the candidate lock, with the locked integer Entrez IDs checked: ABO 28 higher, KIF3C 3797 lower, ZNF835 90485 lower, GLTP 51228 higher, F11 2160 lower. Match the exact single ILMN_GENE symbol after outer whitespace removal. Require each matching probe to have a valid unique ILMN identifier, a finite coefficient and nominal p-value in [0,1]. Preserve every eligible probe per candidate. Do not use alias searches, substitute probes, target ranks from the prior cohorts, binary-expression Table S21, eQTLs, or alternate diagnosis contrasts. Require the source workbook SHA-256 b83a3d32a5882f750d978f4de1a6473fdd5d7627dbccf7d7c1774162e104e8e3, the expected S20 title/header and exactly 15,262 distinct probe rows. A feature omitted by the authors' expression filter is untested in this study.

## Statistical decision

Test each of the five nominated genes for any condition-associated difference using the authors' two-sided nominal `P.Value`. If the paper supplies m eligible probes for a gene, use min(1, m × the smallest nominal p-value). Require Holm adjustment across the original family of **five** genes. Assign p=1 to an untested gene only for multiplicity bookkeeping and explicitly label it unmeasured. Publish every matching probe's ID, reported coefficient, p-value and each tested gene's within-gene and Holm p-values. Assess two-sided targeted support at Holm ≤ 0.05. The statistic differs from the rank-based Finnish and Rome measures. Do not convert the coefficients to percentile points or pool across cohorts.

Reporting direction additionally requires source evidence for which diagnosis is the numerator or reference. Accept a source-defined model contrast, original coefficient coding or independently documented sign anchor; a title listing cases and controls is insufficient. If orientation is verified, report condition-oriented coefficients, note within-gene probe discordance, and compare the signs of all matching probes with the locked Finnish direction. If orientation is unresolved, retain the source coefficient under its original label and report directional agreement as unassessable, regardless of nominal significance. The two-sided test remains interpretable. No retroactive claim of passing the first discovery/validation gate.

The study uses a cached public CC BY 4.0 workbook (4,476,677 bytes). Require separate implementations to verify sheet shape, candidates, probe membership, p-values and Holm denominator. Budget 30 minutes, 1 GB RAM and at most 6 MB of public source transfer for this follow-up. Only aggregate research statistics enter the repository.
