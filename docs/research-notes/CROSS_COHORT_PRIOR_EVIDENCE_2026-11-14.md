# Prior evidence for the cross-cohort endometrial screen

## Prior use of the same human material

Golinska and colleagues included both GSE141549 and GSE25628 in a nine-dataset network meta-analysis of lesions, affected uterine endometrium and comparator uterine endometrium. Table 2 lists 49 affected and 21 comparator uterine samples for the Finnish series, with another 79 lesions. Their major functional enrichment used the intersection of genes identified in two lesion contrasts. Their uterine case–control analysis was separate. Their Rome counts across tissue classes differ from the original source and the submitted labels used here. These are overlapping data and different analytic designs. [Primary study, Table 2 and Results](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2025.1619434) · [Rome source and sampling design](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcp.24358)

Zhang and colleagues performed gene-level and pathway analyses on GSE25628. Their Figure 1F uses the same eight affected uterine biopsies and six comparator biopsies as the Rome validation arm in this project. Their lesion contrast in Figure 1E uses a different tissue. A concordant finding from their uterine comparison would provide methodological corroboration in the same women. It could not add an independent cohort. [Primary study, Figure 1 and Methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2025.114164)

The present Finnish screen tests cases against controls within seven native assay-run and histologic-phase groups on one verified chip. Its publicly locked nominations are tested in Rome. Matching an earlier disease, tissue, gene name or pathway label alone does not establish agreement on a contrast or direction. Whole-tissue measurements can also reflect differences in tissue composition. [Prespecified design](CROSS_COHORT_DISCOVERY_PLAN_2026-11-14.md)

## Evidence specific to the locked nominees

This review began after the [public candidate lock](https://github.com/dovvnloading/Open-Disease/commit/f2a3755ff055cde241e0eaaaedc11a1471270beb). The comparisons below distinguish the molecule assayed, tissue, disease comparison and level of measurement.

| Nominated feature | Primary evidence and relation to the nominated endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| Interleukin-37 signaling | Jiang et al. measured IL-37 by qPCR and immunohistochemistry in ovarian endometriosis and controls. Both uterine and lesion tissue had higher IL-37 RNA and protein than comparator uterine tissue. This is direct prior evidence for IL-37 in the relevant tissue. Our endpoint averages the relative abundance of 19 annotated transcripts, including IL37. Ligand expression alone cannot validate that score or establish pathway activity. [Primary human tissue study](https://doi.org/10.1177/1933719115602775) |
| Signaling by FGFR3 and PI-3K cascade:FGFR3 | A 2026 computational study identified FGFR3 among genes shared by infertile endometriosis and ovarian clear cell carcinoma using uterine arrays from GSE120103. A separate human study assayed FGFR3 RNA in ovarian lesions and control uterine tissue. Neither evaluated these two pathway scores in the matched uterine contrast. All 18 covered genes in the PI-3K set belong to the 38-gene FGFR3 set (Jaccard 0.474). Their nominations are consequently correlated. [Endometriosis–carcinoma network study](https://doi.org/10.22074/ijfs.2026.2067490.1925) · [Lesion study, Figure 5C](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9945179/) · [GSE120103 slide audit](GSE120103_FOSB_RESULTS_2026-11-14.md) |
| Triglyceride catabolism | A study of endometrial fluid in 12 ovarian-endometriosis cases and 23 controls found lower levels of saturated triacylglycerols. The lipid composition of fluid and the relative RNA ranks of the 21 genes in our tissue score measure different quantities. Neither directly measures catabolic flux. [Primary human lipidomics study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28340140/) |
| Golgi Associated Vesicle Biogenesis | A human stromal-cell study reported numerous secretory vesicles around the Golgi in cells from women with endometriosis. This supports prior attention to this biology. Microscopy does not establish the direction of the 43-gene bulk uterine RNA score. [Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-026-00447-w) |
| ABO | Case–control studies have examined ABO blood type and endometriosis, with differing results across populations. Blood type and disease susceptibility are distinct from the prespecified within-tissue RNA measurement. We did not identify a directly comparable primary transcript result in the screened records. [Case–control report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19301022/) · [Laparoscopic cohort](https://doi.org/10.22074/ijfs.2018.5435) |
| KIF3C, ZNF835, GLTP and F11 | We found no primary human report measuring these named transcripts against the same uterine case–control endpoint in the records screened. The gene F11 encodes coagulation factor XI. A search result involving F11R refers to a different gene. The breadth of existing transcriptomic supplements leaves priority unresolved. |

## Search scope

On November 14, 2026 we searched Europe PMC titles and abstracts for endometriosis or endometriotic together with each candidate gene symbol, full gene name, or pathway phrase and common spellings. We used a publication cutoff of November 14, 2026, inspected the returned records, and supplemented these with targeted primary-paper and full-text searches for the tissue, disease, and candidate terms. The following are retrieval counts, including non-primary and incidental mentions. They do not count independent studies or all evidence.
