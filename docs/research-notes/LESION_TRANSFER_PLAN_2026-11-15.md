# Ovarian-to-extraovarian lesion transcriptome transfer

**Local analysis specification, 15 November 2026.** We wrote this before computing any genome-wide lesion-to-uterus results or inspecting the selected genes. Five separately selected genetic anchors had already been examined by the team. Exclude all five from the transfer universe. These two public series have previously been co-analyzed, so this is retrospective secondary research.

Use the ten GSE7305 ovarian endometriosis donor pairs for discovery and the nine GSE11691 peritoneal or broad-ligament pairs for testing. Verify native donor keys as in the genetic-anchor script. Retain IDs that map unambiguously to the same positive Entrez gene on both platforms. Primary scoring uses all clean probes for each common gene on its own platform. Rank all array features within each sample using midranks and express on a 0–100 percentile scale. Average probes per gene; compute lesion minus matched uterus for each donor. Gene inclusion uses annotations only.

1. **Primary global endpoint.** For each common gene use its mean discovery paired difference as its signed weight. Divide weights by their summed absolute values. In validation, compute the weighted sum of paired differences per donor and test whether the average signed transfer is positive with exhaustive within-pair swaps. Under the sharp null that lesion/uterus labels are exchangeable within validation donors, use the one-sided upper tail, alpha 0.05. The unit is the donor. Compute the exact two-sided sign test as a sensitivity to magnitude and the validation leave-one-pair-out range. Refit weights omitting each discovery pair separately. Report the transcriptome-wide Pearson and Spearman correlations of cohort mean differences as descriptors.
2. **Five-gene panel.** Before opening the validation expression matrix, select two largest positive and two most negative effects, then the remaining largest absolute effect, with ties broken by increasing Entrez ID. Use equal weights with discovery signs. Save only identities, directions and discovery effects to a local lock. Test the panel as a secondary global endpoint. Check all five individual validation changes with directional exact sign flips and Holm adjustment within the five. These are validation p-values for a discovery-selected panel.
3. **Probe and scale sensitivity.** Recompute within-sample percentiles using only probe IDs physically shared by both platforms and mapped identically. Refit whole-transcriptome weights on that universe and repeat the same global test. Keep primary panel membership and directions. Apply a donor sign-only score to each gene, aggregate as above using primary weights, and compare direction. Quantify dependence on highly weighted genes by removing the top 1% of absolute primary weights and renormalizing.
4. **Gene-wise shared direction.** For each gene, compute maximum one-sided p over the two studies in each direction, take twice the smaller of the two maxima, cap at 1, then Holm-correct over all genes. Report raw tail resolution and counts passing. Use all 1,024 discovery and 512 validation label permutations. We interpret the gene-wise family separately from the primary global test.

No sample-level expression is published. The signed score compares tissue types in participants with endometriosis. Tissue composition and normal tissue of origin remain competing explanations. No patient diagnosis, mechanistic, prognostic or treatment claim follows from this design.
