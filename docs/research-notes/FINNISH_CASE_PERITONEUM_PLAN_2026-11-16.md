# Frozen case-paired test of the five-gene score within Finnish peritoneal tissue

**Prospective protocol, November 16, 2026.** This is one narrower experiment in GSE141549. In cases with a surgically labelled superficial lesion and a separate peritoneal specimen, does the signed score rise in the lesion? The estimand is the donor-weighted mean lesion-minus-case-peritoneum difference among eligible cases. The earlier eight-donor Finnish result concerned surgically negative controls and different tissues. The six-donor Tan result concerned attached margins in a different study. Both results were known before this specification. Finnish case-pair target signals have not been inspected in this audit. ## Inference and falsification

Use the unweighted mean of each donor's lesion-minus-peritoneum difference as the single primary statistic. Enumerate all 128 within-donor sign assignments. The two-sided p-value is the fraction whose absolute summed difference is at least as large as observed, including ties. Report the mean, positive/zero/negative donor counts, and a 95% common additive shift compatibility interval from inversion of the same test. This model assumes independent donors and exchangeable signs after centering. Tissue labels were observed. Five donors share one processing run, so residual tissue-by-run effects could violate this assumption. No covariate fit or outcome-dependent exclusions. There is one primary endpoint and no gene-wise tests. [Exact interval implementation](../../scripts/normal_peritoneum_score.py)

Report prespecified descriptive checks alongside the primary estimate. Give the range of leave-one-donor-out means and the mean after leaving out the five-donor run, identifying that the latter contains only two donors. Publish no per-donor differences. Check that identical synthetic arrays yield zero and swapping tissue labels reverses the signed effect while preserving the two-sided tail. Use an independent sorted-feature rank calculation and an independently implemented tail and interval to verify the aggregate. A failed software control blocks a result. The earlier noncase peritoneum–uterus comparison remains the biological negative control for interpreting an organ contrast. It is excluded from estimation here.

Even a large, reproducible positive difference cannot qualify a histologically negative control, match a subsite or establish a patient-level diagnostic test. An external analysis must retain its own design, effect and uncertainty. A difference between two publications' mean shifts would add a cohort, platform and sampling contrast.
